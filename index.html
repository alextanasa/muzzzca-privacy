Muzzzca — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 21, 2026

Muzzzca ("the App") is developed by an independent developer ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the App collects and how it is used.

Information We Collect

Muzzzca does not require an account, sign-in, or any personal information to play. The App does not use analytics and does not send gameplay data anywhere.

All game progress — levels completed, stars earned, coins, settings, unlocked items, training history — is stored only on your device, using standard local device storage. This data is never transmitted to us or to any third party.

In-App Purchases

Muzzzca offers one optional, one-time purchase ("Remove Ads"), processed entirely through the App Store's own purchasing system (Apple StoreKit). We do not receive, see, or store your payment information — that is handled directly by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy. This purchase is protected by a parental gate (a simple math challenge) so a young child cannot complete it unassisted.

Advertising

At this time, Muzzzca does not display any third-party advertisements. If ads are introduced in a future update, this policy will be updated in advance, and any ad network used will be one specifically compliant with Apple's Kids Category requirements — contextual (not behavioral/targeted) advertising, human-reviewed ad creative, and no transmission of personally identifiable information or device identifiers to third parties.

Children's Privacy

Muzzzca is designed for young children and does not knowingly collect personal information from any user, whether a child or an adult. A parental gate protects the app's one adult-facing area (the Parent Zone, which shows progress statistics and settings) and any purchase action.

Data Retention & Deletion

Because all data lives only on your device, you can delete it at any time by deleting the app, or by using your device's standard "reset app data" / "offload app" controls. We do not hold a copy anywhere else to delete on your behalf.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes — for example, when a Kids-Category-compliant ad network is added — we will update the "Last updated" date above and the change will ship with that app update.
